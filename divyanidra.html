The Story

Bhakti for Every Moment of Your Day

Divya Nidra — meaning "divine sleep" in Sanskrit — is the devotional sub-label of Roshmusik, born from a single intention: that sacred sound, when crafted with care, becomes medicine for the modern soul.

Across Tamil, Malayalam, Telugu, Hindi, and Sanskrit traditions, our music carries the wisdom of centuries-old chants into a form that meets you where you are — at sunrise sadhana, in the quiet of a long evening, or at the threshold of sleep.

From bhajans to mantras, from short morning blessings to hours-long sleep meditations, every release is produced at Roshmusik Studios in Thrissur, Kerala — where ancient traditions meet contemporary craft.
